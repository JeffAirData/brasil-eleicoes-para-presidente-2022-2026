Who is who

Lula (Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva)
Workers' Party (PT), left. President from 2003 to 2010 and again since 2023.
Jair Bolsonaro
Liberal Party (PL), right. President from 2019 to 2022; lost the 2022 runoff to Lula.
Flávio Bolsonaro
Liberal Party (PL), right. Senator for Rio de Janeiro and Jair Bolsonaro's eldest son; the PL candidate in 2026.
Ronaldo Caiado
Social Democratic Party (PSD). Former governor of Goiás; a presidential candidate in the 2026 first round.
